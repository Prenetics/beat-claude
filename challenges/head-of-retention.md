## The Challenge

You've just signed your offer. It's Day 1.

IM8 today: **subscription is ~75% of revenue**. The active subscriber base is **~210,000 globally**, weighted **78% monthly, 17% quarterly, 5% annual**. **Daily Ultimate Essentials** is the anchor SKU and accounts for **~85% of subscriber baskets**. New Customer AOV is **$160**. **First-purchase ROAS is 0.8x** — the model only works because of retention compounding.

Cohort math today:
- **M1 voluntary churn (after first delivery): 28%**
- **M2–M6 churn: stabilises at ~7%/month**
- **Involuntary churn (failed payments): ~3.5%/month** — meaningfully above category benchmark
- **Net Revenue Retention (12-month): 92%**
- **12-month LTV: $480** (~3x AOV)
- **Cross-sell rate to a second SKU within 6 months: 11%**
- **Reactivation rate (cancelled → re-subscribed within 90 days): 6%**

You inherit a team of **5**: 2 lifecycle managers (one EU, one US), 1 subscription ops manager, 1 copywriter, 1 retention analyst. **No dedicated AI/automation engineer, no community lead, no loyalty product owner.**

Stack today: **Shopify**, **Recharge** (subscription), **Klaviyo** (email + SMS), **Attentive** (US SMS), **Gorgias** (CS), **Looker** (reporting), **Segment** (CDP). AI tools are present in pockets but **not embedded in the operating model** — generative copy is in Klaviyo, but predictive churn, journey orchestration, and content personalisation are still mostly manual.

The CEO's mandate for your first 12 months:

> **"Take NRR from 92% to category-defining. Extend LTR to the point where our paid team can outbid anyone, anywhere. Make this an AI-native retention function — every lifecycle manager working with an AI co-pilot, every journey personalised at scale, every cancellation reason fed back into the loop. Compound the cohorts we already have into a franchise."**

Answer the five parts below.

## Part 1: The NRR Move (30%)

### What to do

The CEO wants NRR from **92% → category-defining**. Tell us exactly how you'd take **5 points of NRR** in the next 12 months. Be specific about sequence, owners, and measurement.

### Your plan must cover

- **The diagnosis.** Looking at the cohort numbers above, where is the actual leak? M1 churn? Involuntary? Quarterly downgrade? Cross-sell drop-off? Show us the math, not the vibes.
- **The top 3 levers, ranked.** For each: what you'd do, the expected NRR contribution in points, the timeline, and what would falsify your bet.
- **The offer architecture.** What changes to the monthly/quarterly/annual mix do you push? Show us the new offer + the migration mechanic to get there.
- **Involuntary churn.** 3.5%/month is leaving money on the table. What's your specific 90-day plan to halve it?
- **The measurement plan.** What's on the weekly dashboard. What incrementality tests prove the levers worked (vs. just correlated with seasonality).

### IM8-specific angles to consider

- **43 markets, 60% non-US.** A one-size-fits-all journey will leave NRR points on the table. What changes per region?
- **Daily Ultimate Essentials is 85% of baskets.** Single-SKU subscribers churn harder than multi-SKU subscribers in supplements. Read the math.
- **Athlete roster is unique.** How do you turn Beckham / Sabalenka / Giannis / Bearman into retention assets, not just acquisition assets?

## Part 2: The AI-Native Operating System (20%)

### What to do

Show us the **specific AI workflows** you'd embed in the IM8 retention function in the first 90 days that materially change either the volume, the cost, or the quality of retention output.

### For each workflow

- **The tool** you'd use (named).
- **The workflow** it replaces or compounds.
- **The retention metric** it moves — and by how much.
- **How you'd measure** whether it's actually working.
- **What could go wrong.**

### What we're NOT looking for

"We'll use ChatGPT to write subject lines" is not an answer. We want operator-grade specificity. At least one workflow should go beyond copy generation — into predictive modelling, journey orchestration, sentiment analysis, or agentic operations. Tie these directly to the three examples from your application.

---

## Part 3: The Cross-Sell Architecture (20%)

### What to do

**11% cross-sell to a second SKU within 6 months is too low** for a brand with our basket potential (Essentials → Longevity → Beckham Stack → future launches). Design the cross-sell engine that doubles it without lifting CAC.

### Your answer must cover

- **The decision tree.** When does an Essentials subscriber become eligible for a Longevity cross-sell? Quarterly upgrade? Bundle? Trial pack?
- **The mechanic.** Sample-in-box? Discounted second SKU? Auto-add at month X? Subscriber-only stack pricing? Defend the mechanic with unit economics.
- **The journey.** What does the lifecycle programme look like for a 6-month subscriber being introduced to their 2nd SKU? Show us the journey, the trigger logic, and the success metric.
- **The team and stack.** Who owns this. What in the stack changes. What new data you need.

---

## Part 4: The Lifecycle System (15%)

### What to do

Sketch the operating system you'd run for the lifecycle programme — email, SMS, push, in-app — across **43 markets**, with AI inside the loop, not bolted on.

### Your plan must cover

- **Cadence & roles.** Who briefs what, when. What gets QA'd by AI, what gets QA'd by a human.
- **The content engine.** How you produce enough localised, personalised lifecycle content for 43 markets without a 50-person team. Where AI does the heavy lifting, where craft is non-negotiable.
- **The test-and-learn rhythm.** What you test weekly, what you test monthly, what you never touch. How a winning test becomes the new default within a week.
- **Deliverability and brand coherence.** How you protect both at this volume.

---

## Part 5: The Hard Tradeoff (15%)

### What to do

Pick **ONE** of these four decisions. Tell us exactly what you'd do, why, and what evidence would change your mind.

**(a)** Your retention analyst says raising M1 retention by 5 points requires a free 7-day onboarding kit (cost: $18/sub). Finance says it adds 6 weeks to payback. The CEO is publicly committed to faster payback. Do you ship the kit?

**(b)** Klaviyo's predictive churn model is right ~70% of the time. Building a custom model on your CDP data would push that to ~85% but takes 4 months and an engineering hire. Do you use Klaviyo's model now, or build the custom one?

**(c)** Annual subscriptions have 3x the LTV of monthly but a 2x higher conversion friction. Your CFO wants you to push 30% of new subscribers onto annual within 6 months. Your data says aggressive annual push will tank acquisition conversion by 15%. What do you commit to?

**(d)** A "skip-the-month" feature would reduce cancellations by an estimated 22% but also reduce monthly revenue by an estimated 8%. Do you ship it?

### What we're looking for

- A decision, not a hedge. "It depends" without saying what it depends on will fail.
- The falsifiable evidence that would make you change your mind.
- Commercial judgement, not optimism.

## What We're Looking For

- **Cohort fluency.** You think in cohorts, not campaigns. You can read the numbers above and tell us a story before we tell you ours.
- **Commercial rigour.** Real numbers, real math, real tradeoffs. If you cite a benchmark, cite the source.
- **AI in the bones.** Not "we could use AI for this." Show us what you'd build, with what tool, on what timeline, measured how.
- **Specificity over surface area.** A 4-page memo with sharp answers beats a 6-page memo with hedges. Vague answers get scored down hard.
- **Builder energy.** This is a build job, not an inherit job. Show us how you operate when there is no playbook.
