**IM8 Health | Prenetics (NASDAQ: PRE)**
*Co-founded by David Beckham*

---

## The Challenge

We gave Claude this exact brief. It produced a solid, structured, technically competent response.

**Your job is to beat it.**

Claude is good at frameworks, rate cards, and tiered commission structures. What it can't do is spot the next breakout creator before they blow up, negotiate a whitelisting deal that actually converts, or figure out why your TikTok Shop affiliate programme stalled at 200 creators when it should be at 1,000.

This challenge is designed to find people who can.

## About IM8

IM8 is a premium nutritional supplement brand co-founded by David Beckham. Our flagship product — **Daily Ultimate Essentials** — delivers 92 clinically dosed nutrients in a single daily serving. NSF Certified for Sport, backed by a 12-week randomized controlled trial. ~$120M ARR, targeting $250M across DTC subscription, Amazon, and TikTok Shop.

Our ambassador roster: **David Beckham** (co-founder, 87M followers), **Aryna Sabalenka** (WTA #1), **Oliver Bearman** (F1, Haas), **Giannis Antetokounmpo** (NBA, Milwaukee Bucks), and **Inter Miami CF** (official hydration partner).

We compete with AG1, Bloom, and Ritual. We have the strongest roster in the category, a category-defining product, and the budget. What we need is a leader who turns that into a measurable, always-on creator engine.

## The Role

You will own IM8's influencer, creator, and affiliate strategy globally. Three muscles: **paid creator** (whitelisted, sparked, licensed content for paid social), **organic ambassador** (athletes, coaches, doctors, tastemakers), and **affiliate** (TikTok Shop, Amazon Influencer, Social Snowball). You report to the VP of Growth and sit on the senior growth leadership team.

**This is NOT a brand marketing role.** This is about building a revenue-generating creator engine with a P&L you own. We want to see how you'd actually build it, not how you'd present it in a deck.

## Part 1: TikTok Shop Affiliate Engine (35%)

### Scenario

IM8 has zero TikTok Shop affiliates today. You've been given 90 days and the budget to build a 1,000-creator programme from scratch. The VP of Growth wants this channel generating $1.5M/month in GMV by month 3.

### What We Need From You

**A. Recruitment Strategy**

Walk us through how you'd recruit 1,000 creators in 90 days. Be specific:
- Where do you find them? What tools do you use for discovery?
- What's your outreach sequence — first touch, follow-up, conversion?
- What's your acceptance criteria? What makes someone an IM8 affiliate vs. not?
- How do you scale past the first 100 without quality dropping off a cliff?
- What's your target acceptance rate, and what does that mean for total outreach volume?

**B. Commission & Incentive Structure**

Design the full commission structure. Present as a table:

| Tier | Criteria | Commission | Bonuses | Perks |
|------|----------|------------|---------|-------|

Include:
- How creators move between tiers
- What happens when a creator goes dormant?
- How do you prevent commission gaming?
- What's the blended commission rate you're targeting, and why?

**C. Content Playbooks**

Give us 5 specific content formats that work for supplement brands on TikTok Shop. For each:
- Format name and description
- Why it converts (not just entertains)
- How you'd brief a creator on it
- Example hook line

**D. Retention & Churn**

Your programme is live and growing. But at month 2, you notice 30% of creators haven't posted in 3 weeks. Walk us through:
- How do you identify at-risk creators before they go silent?
- What's your re-engagement playbook?
- What's an acceptable churn rate for a programme this size?
- How do you build a system so this doesn't require your personal attention at scale?

## Part 2: Athlete & Ambassador Activation (25%)

### Scenario

You've inherited these athlete contracts. Each has remaining content deliverables, usage rights, and social amplification obligations. Your job is to turn contracted assets into compounding content — not just one-off posts.

### What We Need From You

**Pick 3 of our 5 athlete assets** (Beckham, Sabalenka, Bearman, Giannis, Inter Miami) and for each:

**A. 90-Day Content Plan**
- What content are you producing, and in what formats?
- How many shoot days do you need, and what do you capture in each?
- How do you maximise one shoot day into 6 months of content?
- What's the distribution strategy across owned, paid, and creator channels?

**B. Creator Crossover**
- How do you connect this athlete's content to your affiliate programme?
- Can creators use athlete content? Under what terms?
- Give us one specific campaign idea that combines an athlete + 50 creators + TikTok Shop

**C. Measurement**
- How do you prove that athlete content drives revenue, not just impressions?
- What attribution model do you use?
- What does "underperformance" look like for a tier-1 athlete, and what do you do about it?

We're NOT looking for "Beckham posts a reel." We want to see how you'd turn these assets into a content supply chain.

## Part 3: Commercial Framework & P&L (20%)

### Scenario

The VP of Growth has asked you to present your annual influencer budget and projected return. You have access to an eight-figure total budget. Defend how you'd allocate it.

### What We Need From You

**A. Budget Allocation**

Present your annual budget split across:

| Category | % of Budget | Annual Spend | Expected Return | ROAS |
|----------|-------------|--------------|-----------------|------|

Include: paid creator, TikTok Shop affiliate, ambassador activation, tools/ops, team costs

**B. Rate Cards**

Design the rate card you'd use for paid creator partnerships. Present as a table by tier (nano through macro/celebrity). Include:
- Per-post and per-video rates
- Whitelisting/usage rights pricing
- Exclusivity premiums
- What you'd NEVER pay for, and why

**C. Reporting & Accountability**

- What does your weekly report to the VP of Growth look like? Give us the actual format.
- What are the 5 numbers you'd put on a dashboard that the CEO sees?
- How do you handle a month where you miss your NCR target by 30%?

## What We're Evaluating

- **Operator mindset** — Can you actually build and run this, or do you just strategise about it?
- **Commercial brain** — Do you think in CAC, LTV, ROAS, and contribution margin? Or do you pitch reach decks?
- **Creator instinct** — Do you understand what makes content convert on TikTok Shop, not just get views?
- **Talent experience** — Have you dealt with agents, managers, and the realities of athlete partnerships?
- **Specificity** — Real rate cards, real numbers, real timelines. Not frameworks and buzzwords.

## What We're NOT Looking For

- Generic influencer marketing playbooks
- "Best practice" frameworks without IM8-specific context
- Strategy decks — this is a build role, not a consulting engagement
- Responses that read like they were generated by AI without adding real operational experience
- Reach and impression metrics without revenue attribution
