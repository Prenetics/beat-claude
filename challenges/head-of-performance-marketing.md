## The Challenge

You've just signed your offer. It's Day 1.

IM8 currently spends **$2.5M/month** on global paid acquisition. CAC is **$200**, blended ROAS is **0.8x first-purchase**, payback sits at **~6 months**. New Customer AOV is **$160**, subscription first purchase rate is **75%**, 12-month LTV is **3x**. Mix today: **Meta 65% / Google 20% / TikTok 10% / AppLovin 5%**. Creative refresh cadence is roughly **4,000 ads/month**. You inherit a team of 4 (2 channel managers, 2 in-house editors, no strategist).

The CEO has given you a clear mandate for the next 12 months:

> **"Double new customer acquisition. Improve CAC. Get payback under 3 months. Be the best-run performance team I've ever seen."**

You have ONE budget ask you can put in front of the CMO and CEO in your first 30 days. After that, you operate within whatever they approve.

Submit a strategy memo (max 6 pages — yes, write fewer pages, not more) that answers the five parts below.

---

## Part 1: The 30-Day Plan (40%)

### What to do

Sequence your first 30 days to set the stage for hitting the CEO's mandate. We want to see what you'd actually do day-to-day in your first month — not a vague 12-month vision.

### Your plan must cover

- **Sequence & milestones:** How would you sequence the next 30 days? Be specific about phases, the order of decisions, and what you would and wouldn't try in month one.
- **Channel mix shift:** Where does the growth actually come from? Channel-by-channel projection: what % of incremental new customers comes from each channel by the end of 30 days, and why.
- **Creative volume bet:** What's your specific bet on creative volume and variety? How many ads/week, produced how, and how do you measure whether the volume is actually winning?

### IM8-specific angles to consider

- The CAC ($200), payback (6mo), and ROAS (0.8x first-purchase) numbers tell a story about where the leverage is. Read the math.
- Meta is 65% of spend today. Is that a problem or a feature?
- The team has no strategist. What does that constraint mean for what you can ship in 30 days?

---

## Part 2: The One Budget Ask (20%)

### What to do

You only get ONE budget ask in your first 30 days. State it in one sentence. Then defend it.

### Your answer must cover

- **The ask itself:** One sentence. Specific.
- **What it unlocks:** What the ROI math looks like — show the unit economics, not vibes.
- **The trade-off:** What you'd cut from the current $2.5M/month if the ask gets declined.

We want to see how you think under commercial constraint. Multiple asks disguised as one will be scored down.

---

## Part 3: The Hard Tradeoffs (15%)

### What to do

Pick **ONE** of these three decisions. Tell us exactly what you'd do, why, and what evidence would change your mind.

**(a)** AppLovin is pitching you on $200K/month minimum spend at projected $140 CAC. Meta CAC is creeping toward $220. Do you take the AppLovin deal?

**(b)** Your in-house editors can produce 600 ads/week at 70% of agency quality. The agency produces 400/week at 100%. Do you fire the agency?

**(c)** Your MMM says Meta is being over-credited by 30% vs last-click. Your CFO is using last-click numbers in the board deck. What do you do this week, this month, this quarter?

### What we're looking for

- A decision, not a hedge. "It depends" without saying what it depends on will fail.
- The falsifiable evidence that would make you change your mind.
- Commercial judgement, not optimism.

---

## Part 4: Creative System Design (15%)

### What to do

Sketch the operating system you'd run between channel data and creative output. We want to see how you'd actually run the loop.

### Your answer must cover

- **Cadence & roles:** Who does what, when. What meetings, what artifacts, what metrics.
- **Brief-to-ship flow:** What gets briefed when, and how a winning ad gets compounded into more winning ads.
- **A concrete example:** What does "creative learnings as compounding advantage" actually look like in practice? Give a real example from a previous role or a hypothetical week.

---

## Part 5: AI as Edge, Not Buzzword (10%)

### What to do

Show us **THREE specific AI workflows** you would deploy in the first 90 days that materially change either the volume, the cost, or the quality of performance marketing output at IM8.

### For each workflow

- **The tool** you'd use (named).
- **The workflow** it replaces or compounds.
- **How you'd measure** whether it's actually working.
- **What could go wrong.**

### What we're NOT looking for

"We'll use ChatGPT to write ad copy" is not an answer. We want operator-grade specificity. At least one of your three workflows should go beyond copy or creative generation.

---

## What We're Looking For

- **Strategic depth.** Show us you've thought past the obvious. The obvious answer to "double acquisition" is "spend more on Meta." We want to see what you'd do that's NOT that.
- **Commercial rigour.** Real numbers, real math, real tradeoffs. If you cite a benchmark, cite the source.
- **Specificity over surface area.** A 4-page memo with sharp answers beats a 6-page memo with hedges. Vague answers get scored down hard.
- **Builder energy.** This is a build job, not an inherit job. Show us how you operate when there is no playbook.
