# Beat Claude Challenge Brief

## Intermediate Graphic Designer — IM8 / Prenetics

**Format:** Take-home design exercise  
**Turnaround:** 10 hours from your agreed start time  
**Expected working time:** 3–4 hours maximum  
**Deliverables:** Three finished designs, editable source files, and a PDF of up to four pages

---

## About This Challenge

Can you turn a simple brief into a polished, consistent campaign—and use AI to improve the result? We want to see your eye, your execution, and the decisions you make under a short deadline. Your submission will be assessed against an AI-assisted baseline using the same brief and scoring criteria. The goal: beat Claude through the quality of your finished work.

## About IM8

IM8 is a premium health and nutrition brand co-founded by David Beckham and Prenetics. Explore [im8health.com](https://im8health.com) for the current visual identity and product imagery. This exercise focuses on IM8, within a role supporting the wider Prenetics brand family.

## The Challenge

IM8 is preparing a fictional pop-up introducing Daily Ultimate Essentials to busy professionals who are new to the brand. Create one visual direction that works across social, email, and an event poster.

The campaign should feel premium, recognizable as IM8, and immediately understandable. The objective is to get someone to visit the pop-up and discover the product.

**Use this exact campaign copy on all three designs:**

- Headline: **Meet your daily ritual.**
- Product: **IM8 Daily Ultimate Essentials**
- Event: **The IM8 Daily Ritual Pop-Up**
- Details: **Saturday, 10 October 2026 | 10am–6pm | Central, Hong Kong**
- CTA: **Discover IM8**
- Website: **im8health.com**

The event is fictional and created solely for this exercise. Do not add discounts, health claims, testimonials, or celebrity imagery. Use the current IM8 logo and product imagery from the website; list your source links. If a font or high-resolution asset is unavailable, use a sensible substitute or the best available image and flag it in your handoff. No paid asset purchase is expected.

## Part 1 — Hero Design (40%)

Create a **1080 × 1350 px social post** featuring the product and all supplied copy.

We will assess typography, hierarchy, spacing, composition, image treatment, attention to detail, and how convincingly the work fits IM8. Present the actual flat artwork; a device mockup is optional and cannot replace it.

## Part 2 — Adapt the System (25%)

Adapt your concept into:

- **Email hero:** 1200 × 800 px, intended to display at 600 px wide. Keep all copy readable at that display size. No HTML required.
- **A3 portrait event poster:** 297 × 420 mm, with 3 mm bleed. Supply a print PDF and briefly state your color-profile choice, image-resolution check, and anything you would confirm with the printer before production.

Recompose for each format. Show that the same design language works at different sizes and viewing distances. Advanced fabrication knowledge is not required; sound print fundamentals and clear assumptions are.

## Part 3 — Show Your AI Judgment (20%)

Use AI for at least one meaningful step, such as visual exploration, background generation, retouching, or quality checks. Any accessible tool is welcome; paid access is not required.

Show one actual prompt, its output or a screenshot, and your final treatment. In up to 150 words, explain what you kept, changed, or rejected and why. If you reject the entire output, show how it informed your final decision. Keep product packaging and logo details accurate.

We assess your judgment and improvement of the result, not the number of tools you use.

## Part 4 — Handoff & Self-Check (15%)

Supply organized, editable files in Figma, Illustrator, Photoshop, or InDesign, with clearly named layers or frames and linked assets where needed. Include digital PNG/JPG exports and the poster PDF.

Add a short handoff note covering:

- Your visual direction and two deliberate choices.
- Fonts, colors, and asset source links.
- Checks completed, any production limitations, and actual time spent.

Keep this note to one page within your submission PDF. We should be able to understand the files and continue your work without guessing.

## What We're Looking For

- Strong design craft and clear information hierarchy.
- Consistency across formats, with thoughtful adaptation.
- AI use that demonstrates an editor's eye.
- Accurate copy, reliable exports, and an orderly handoff.
- Sensible prioritization within the time limit.

## What We're NOT Looking For

- A moodboard or written concept in place of finished designs.
- Three unrelated layouts or simple stretched resizes.
- Uncorrected AI artifacts, altered packaging, or invented claims.
- An elaborate strategy deck or additional unrequested deliverables.

## How to Submit

Agree your start time with recruitment before beginning. Your deadline is exactly **10 hours later**, with both times confirmed in Hong Kong time. This is a submission window, not an expectation to work for 10 hours. Stop after four hours of active work and note any unfinished details.

Email **recruitment@prenetics.com** with subject:

**Beat Claude — Intermediate Graphic Designer — [Your Name]**

Include:

- A PDF of up to four pages showing the three designs and your process/handoff notes.
- A Drive or Dropbox folder containing exports and editable sources, or an accessible Figma link with the exports. Check permissions before sending.
- A Loom walkthrough of no more than two minutes explaining your key decisions; include this within your four-hour working limit.
- Your portfolio and three examples of your existing AI design workflow, if not already submitted with your application.
- Your current and expected salary.

Role details: [Intermediate Graphic Designer](https://careers.prenetics.com/jobs/7792918-intermediate-graphic-designer).

This is the everyday challenge: take a brief, make good design decisions, adapt them across formats, and hand over work the team can use. Show us what your taste and judgment add to the tools.
