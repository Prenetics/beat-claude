## The Challenge

You're our new Paid Social Video Editor. Your first assignment: take existing IM8 assets and cut a platform-native paid ad that stops the thumb and drives conversion — then show us you can spin volume and variation from a single concept, and that you know *why* it works.

**We won't supply any footage — sourcing your own raw material is part of the job.** Pull everything you need from IM8's publicly available content: our ads on the Meta Ad Library, plus our TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and website. Screen-record or download publicly available clips (creator/UGC, ambassador, product, testimonial, b-roll). **Do not shoot anything new.**

## Part 1 — Cut the Hero Ad (35%)

Edit one paid-social ad, 15–30 seconds, built for Meta or TikTok (your call — state which). It must have a strong 3-second hook, a clear narrative, on-brand captions and motion, and an unmistakable CTA. Deliver it in the platform's vertical aspect ratio (9:16), legible both sound-on and sound-off.

---

## Part 2 — The Hook Test / Variation Matrix (25%)

From that same ad, produce 3–4 alternative openings (the first 3–6 seconds recut) that test genuinely different angles — e.g. founder/Beckham, science/"92 nutrients", social proof/testimonial, problem-first. For each, give one line on the angle and who it's aimed at. This is the daily reality of the role: many at-bats from one concept.

---

## Part 3 — Why It Performs, and What You'd Test Next (15%)

One page: why your hook and cut will move thumb-stop rate, watch time, and CTR/CVR; which variant you'd bet on and why; and what you'd test next once the first data comes back. Speak in performance terms, not vibes.

---

## Part 4 — AI in Your Edit Workflow (15%)

Show, don't tell. How is AI actually in your pipeline — scripting/hook ideation, auto-captioning, b-roll or voiceover generation, upscaling, mass-versioning, reframing? Share the real prompts and tools you used on this task, and what you kept versus threw away.

---

## Part 5 — Craft & Presentation (10%)

Editing craft (pacing, sound design, motion, polish), brand fluency, and how clearly you present the work.

---

## What We're Looking For

- A hook that genuinely stops the scroll in the first 3 seconds
- Real direct-response instinct — the edit is built to convert, not just to look pretty
- Volume and variation from one idea, fast
- Premium brand feel held at speed
- A genuine, embedded AI workflow that makes you faster
- Performance literacy — you connect creative choices to metrics
- Resourcefulness — finding and repurposing the right public assets on your own

---

## What We're NOT Looking For

- A polished brand film with no performance intent
- One beautiful edit and zero variations
- Hooks with no rationale, or "it just looks cool"
- A list of AI tools you've heard of but didn't use here
- Ignoring the brief's platform, ratio, or CTA requirements
- Over-leaning on Beckham as a crutch instead of craft
