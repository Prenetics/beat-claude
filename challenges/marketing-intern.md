## The Challenge

You have just joined IM8 as a Marketing Intern.

Your job is simple: **spot something valuable that we are missing, build a practical response, and show us how AI helps you produce better work faster.**

We care less about whether you have done this exact job before. We care about curiosity, initiative, commercial instinct, taste, and whether you can turn AI into real output.

---

## Part 1 — Find the Opportunity (25%)

Review IM8's public marketing ecosystem and identify **one opportunity** you believe IM8 should act on.

It could involve:

- Content or social media
- Creators or partnerships
- A campaign or product launch
- Website conversion
- Community building
- Retention or customer education
- Something we have not listed

### Your answer must cover:

- What you noticed
- The public evidence behind your observation
- Why it matters to IM8 and its audience
- The likely commercial or brand outcome
- What could prove your idea wrong

Do not give us ten generic ideas. Pick one, prioritise it, and commit.

---

## Part 2 — Build the Idea (35%)

Turn your opportunity into something IM8 could actually execute.

### Your answer must include:

- The target audience
- The central idea and why it fits IM8
- Three concrete marketing assets or executions
- A seven-day launch plan
- The people, tools, or inputs you would need
- One primary success metric and one guardrail metric
- The main reason the idea could fail

Show us what you would make, not only what you would recommend. Include at least one tangible artifact: a post, script, visual mock-up, landing-page wireframe, creator shortlist, campaign brief, working tool, or equivalent.

---

## Part 3 — Show Your AI Advantage (30%)

Use AI to help build your submission, then show us the work behind the work.

### Your answer must include:

- Your original prompt
- One weak or generic AI output
- How you corrected or redirected it
- The improved output
- One finished artifact created with AI support
- One repeatable AI workflow that could save the marketing team time every week
- The point where human judgment or fact-checking must take over

We are not scoring how many AI tools you can name. We are scoring how well you direct them, challenge them, and turn their output into work worth shipping.

---

## Part 4 — Hunger & Communication (10%)

Answer these three questions directly:

1. What did you teach yourself to complete this challenge?
2. What did you do that was not explicitly requested?
3. If you joined next Monday, what would you volunteer to own first?

Keep this section specific. We want evidence of initiative, not adjectives about being proactive.

---

## What We're Looking For

- Curiosity and initiative
- Strong internet and cultural awareness
- Commercial thinking, even without perfect data
- Real output rather than presentation theatre
- Comfort learning unfamiliar things quickly
- Evidence that AI makes you materially faster or better
- Judgment about when not to trust AI
- Someone who finds useful work without waiting to be assigned it

---

## What We're NOT Looking For

- A generic marketing audit with no clear decision
- Ten ideas and no prioritisation
- "Post more on TikTok" without a specific audience, asset, or reason
- A list of AI tools with no prompts or outputs
- Polished AI copy that you did not challenge or improve
- Vanity metrics with no connection to a business or customer outcome
- Work that could apply to any brand
- Claims about IM8 that you did not verify
