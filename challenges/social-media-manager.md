## The Challenge

You are the newly hired Social Media Manager at IM8. Your first 30 days are an audit-and-build sprint: understand the existing social footprint, benchmark against the best in wellness and lifestyle, and lay out the social strategy and content engine you'll execute against in your first 90 days.

**Everything you need is publicly available.** Visit IM8's channels. Follow them. Watch ten of their TikToks. Read the comments. Then design something better.

---

## Part 1: Full 360 Social Strategy & Content Architecture (35%)

### What to do

Design IM8's **complete organic social strategy** across all priority platforms. This is your blueprint for what IM8's social presence should look like over the next 12 months.

### Your strategy must cover

- **Channel Mix & Prioritisation:** Which platforms get our attention, in what order, and why? Justify your call with reference to IM8's audience, product, and ambassador roster.
- **Content Pillars:** Define 4–6 content pillars (e.g. science explainers, ambassador stories, ritual / habit content, transformation, behind-the-scenes, education). Show what % of output each pillar gets per week.
- **Format Architecture:** What formats win on each platform — and what does IM8's signature format look like? (Talking-head, doc-style, edu-tainment, ASMR, athlete POV, etc.)
- **Posting Cadence:** Weekly cadence by platform with rationale. Where is IM8 over- or under-investing today?
- **Brand Voice on Social:** How does IM8 sound on TikTok vs LinkedIn vs X? Show the dial settings, with examples.
- **Ambassador Integration:** How do you weave Beckham, Sabalenka, and Bearman into the social feed without making it look like an athlete fan account? Where do they appear, how often, in what role?
- **Community Strategy:** What's the always-on community management programme? Response SLAs, tone, escalation, UGC sourcing.
- **Organic-to-Paid Pipeline:** How does social feed performance marketing? What's the trigger to push an organic post into paid?
- **KPIs & Targets:** What you measure, the benchmark you're targeting, and how you track contribution to brand awareness, brand search, and acquisition.

### IM8-specific angles to consider

- IM8 is a *premium* brand competing against AG1, Ritual, Athletic Greens, Momentous — how do you avoid sounding like a science textbook AND avoid sounding like a meme account?
- 12-week RCT and NSF Certified for Sport are differentiators — how do you make these *social-native* rather than press-release fodder?
- Beckham is the most recognisable co-founder — ambassador over-reliance is a real failure mode. How do you build a brand voice that survives without him?
- Habit formation is the core retention mechanic — how does social reinforce daily ritual?

## Part 2: IM8 Audit + Competitive Benchmarking (25%)

### What to do

Audit IM8's current social presence and benchmark against 3–4 direct or adjacent competitors (e.g. AG1, Ritual, Momentous, Huel, Seed, ARMRA, David Protein).

### Your audit should include

- **Channel-by-channel snapshot:** Followers, engagement rate, posting cadence, top 3 performing posts (with hypothesis on why they worked)
- **Top 3 things IM8 is doing well today**
- **Top 5 things you'd change in your first 30 days**
- **Competitive comparison:** Side-by-side on cadence, format mix, brand voice, ambassador usage, community quality. Screenshots required.
- **Gap analysis:** Where is the white space IM8 should own? What can no competitor credibly claim?

---

## Part 3: Sample Content Drop (20%)

### What to do

Build a **5-piece sample content drop** you would actually ship in your first month at IM8. Across the 5 pieces, cover at least 3 of the content pillars from Part 1, and at least 2 different platforms.

### For each piece, include

- **Platform** (TikTok, IG Reels, YouTube Shorts, etc.)
- **Pillar** it serves
- **Hook** (first 2 seconds — written out)
- **Concept / shot list** (10–15 lines max)
- **Caption** (full)
- **CTA** (or a deliberate decision not to include one — explain)
- **Distribution intent** (organic-only, organic-to-paid pipeline candidate, ambassador re-share, etc.)

We'd rather see 5 specific, ship-tomorrow concepts than 20 vague ideas. Don't tell us about a "behind-the-scenes series" — show us the first three episodes.

---

## Part 4: AI Integration (15%)

### What to do

Demonstrate how you would embed AI across the social workflow at IM8. This is not theoretical — show your actual prompts, outputs, and refinements.

### We want to see

- **Hook & Caption Generation:** Show your real prompts for generating high-performing hooks and captions. Include outputs and how you refined them.
- **Trend Detection:** How do you use AI to surface trends, sounds, and cultural moments before competitors do?
- **Comment & DM Triage:** How would AI help manage community at scale without losing tone?
- **Performance Analysis:** How do you use AI to analyse what's working week-on-week?
- **Prompt Library:** Share 3–5 of your best prompts for social-specific tasks, with notes on when you use each.

We're not looking for a list of AI tools you've heard of. We want to see AI is genuinely embedded in how you work.
