## The Challenge

You've just joined the Paid Social team. You own day-to-day buying for **two markets — the UK and Australia — with a combined budget of ~$95K/week (~$410K/month)** on Meta. You report to the Head of Performance Marketing and partner with a dedicated paid creative team. *(All figures below are illustrative.)*

Some context on the business you're buying for:

- **New Customer AOV:** $135
- **Subscription first-purchase rate:** ~70%
- **12-month LTV:** ~2.5x first order
- **Target blended CAC:** $175 (payback target: under 3 months)
- **Creative supply:** the creative team can ship you ~30 net-new ad concepts/week across both markets

Here's last week's account snapshot for your two markets *(illustrative)*:

| Campaign | Market | Strategy | Spend | New Customers | CAC | First-purchase ROAS | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| UK – Prospecting ABO | UK | Manual, broad | $30,000 | 130 | $231 | 0.68x | 6 ad sets, manual bids, flat 3 weeks |
| UK – Advantage+ Shopping | UK | A+ Shopping | $22,000 | 145 | $152 | 1.03x | Scaled 40% last week, CAC held |
| UK – Retargeting | UK | ABO, 7-day site visitors | $7,000 | 48 | $146 | 1.08x | Tiny audience, frequency 6.8 |
| AU – Prospecting CBO | AU | CBO, 4 ad sets | $22,000 | 88 | $250 | 0.63x | One ad set eating 70% of budget |
| AU – Advantage+ Shopping | AU | A+ Shopping | $10,000 | 56 | $179 | 0.88x | Launched 10 days ago, learning |
| AU – Creative Test | AU | ABO, test cell | $4,000 | 10 | $400 | 0.39x | 3 new concepts, 4 days live |

You have **no incremental budget this week** — same ~$95K/week. The Head of Performance wants your read and your plan by end of day.

Answer the four parts below.

---

## Part 1 — Read the Account & Make the Calls (30%)

This is the core of the role. Look at the table above and tell us exactly what you'd do.

Your answer must cover:

- **The diagnosis.** In a few tight sentences: where is the money working, where is it leaking, and what's the single biggest problem in this account right now?
- **The reallocation.** You have the same total budget. Move it. Be specific — which campaigns get cut, which get scaled, by how much, and to what new daily/weekly spend. Show the before/after.
- **The blended-CAC math.** What does your reallocation do to blended CAC across the two markets vs. last week? Show the arithmetic, not a vibe.
- **What you'd watch.** What are the two or three numbers you'd check in the first 72 hours to know your calls are working — and what reading would make you reverse a decision?

We're looking for someone who reads CAC against LTV and payback, not last-click ROAS in isolation. Note that first-purchase ROAS of 1.0x on a 75% subscription business is not the same as 1.0x on a one-off purchase — show us you understand why.

---

## Part 2 — The Creative Test Plan (25%)

Creative is the biggest lever in paid social. The creative team can give you ~30 new concepts/week. Show us how you'd actually use them.

Your answer must cover:

- **A structured 2-week test plan** for one of your two markets: how many concepts, what variables you're isolating (angle, hook, format, offer), how you'd structure the cells so the read is clean, and what budget you'd ring-fence for testing vs. scaling proven winners.
- **What "winning" means.** The exact metric and threshold a new concept must clear to graduate from test to scale — and how long / how much spend you'd give it before you call it.
- **One real creative brief.** Write the actual brief you'd hand the creative team for ONE new concept — angle, hook, who it's for, the proof point it leans on, the format, and the call to action. One paragraph, the way you'd really write it.

---

## Part 3 — The Tactical Tradeoff (25%)

Pick **ONE** of the three decisions below. Tell us exactly what you'd do, why, and what evidence would change your mind.

**(a)** Your UK Advantage+ Shopping campaign is your best performer ($152 CAC) and you scaled it 40% last week without CAC moving. Do you scale it another 40% this week, or hold? What would make you wrong?

**(b)** The AU Creative Test cell is at $400 CAC after 4 days and $4,000 spent — well above target. Do you kill it now, or keep it live? At what point is "give creative time to learn" just burning money?

**(c)** Your UK Retargeting is your lowest CAC ($146) but frequency is 6.8 and the audience is tiny. Do you push more budget into it, hold, or pull back? What's the trap here?

We want a decision, not a hedge. "It depends" without saying what it depends on will fail.

---

## Part 4 — How You Work With AI (20%)

IM8 is an AI-native team. We expect you to use AI every day to move faster than a buyer who doesn't.

Your answer must cover:

- **Two real examples** of how you currently use AI in your buying workflow today — the tool/stack, the exact task it replaces or compounds, and the measured impact (time saved, tests shipped, CAC moved). Proof — a link, screenshot, or Loom — beats description.
- **One AI workflow you'd build** in your first 30 days at IM8 to do this job better. Name the tool/stack, the workflow it replaces, how you'd measure whether it's working, and what could go wrong.

"I use ChatGPT to write ad copy" is not an example. What was the prompt, what was the output, what did it do for performance?

## What We're Looking For

- **You read the math.** CAC against LTV and payback, not ROAS in a vacuum. You spot the leaking campaign and the over-credited winner.
- **Decisiveness under constraint.** You move budget and defend it. You make the tradeoff call.
- **Clean test design.** Your creative tests isolate one variable and have a real graduation threshold.
- **Specificity over surface area.** A sharp 2-page answer beats a padded 3-page one. Vague answers get scored down hard.
- **AI-native, not AI-curious.** Your workflow shows AI inside the loop, with proof.

---

## What We're NOT Looking For

- A reallocation with no arithmetic — "shift budget to the winners" is not an answer
- Reading first-purchase ROAS as if this were a one-off-purchase business
- "Test, learn, iterate" without naming what gets tested, learned, or killed
- "It depends" on Part 3 — pick one and defend it
- AI examples without proof — links, screenshots, or Looms are expected in Part 4
- Padding, hedging, or anything that reads like a consultant deliverable instead of a buyer's working notes
