## The Challenge

You've just been hired as Head of Content. Here's your operating reality *(illustrative)*:

- IM8 runs a full-portfolio content operation: thousands of paid social assets live at any time, alongside TV/brand film, out-of-home (including 3D anamorphic billboards), ambassador and athlete campaign content, organic social, email/CRM, retail and event creative, and PR assets.
- The content team must ship **~5,000 assets/month** across all of these channels.
- Your core team is **5–10 editors**, extended by agencies, freelancers, creators, and AI. Leading, developing, and quality-controlling this editor team is a core part of the job.
- Ambassador content (Beckham, Giannis, Sabalenka, Bearman, Inter Miami CF and others) runs on fixed shoot windows and strict approval chains — you get limited time with talent and the output must feed every channel.
- The media-buying and organic social teams sit in Hong Kong. You'll partner with them daily — so if you're US-based, meaningful overlap with HK hours is required.

Your first 30 days are a build-and-diagnose sprint. Answer the five parts below.

---

## Part 1 — The Content Engine: Operating Across the Full Portfolio (30%)

Design the operating system that reliably ships ~5,000 assets/month across paid social, TV/brand film, out-of-home, ambassador campaigns, organic, email/CRM, retail and PR — with a core team of 5–10 editors plus agencies/freelancers/creators/AI.

- The end-to-end workflow: brief → production → review/approval → versioning → localization → compliance/claims check → distribution → measurement. Show where editors, freelancers, agencies, and AI each sit — and how the workflow differs between high-volume channels (paid social) and high-stakes channels (TV, billboards, ambassador films).
- Portfolio prioritization: how you allocate finite editor capacity across always-on performance content, tentpole brand moments (TV, OOH, launches), and ambassador shoot windows that can't move.
- Asset multiplication: how one ambassador shoot or one brand film becomes hundreds of assets across paid, organic, CRM, OOH and PR — your versioning and repurposing system.
- Quality at volume: how brand quality, health-claim integrity, and originality survive 5,000/month across channels with very different quality bars.
- The bottleneck problem: how the team moves fast without you approving every asset — what you personally review vs. delegate, and the guardrails that make that safe.

---

## Part 2 — Performance Creative System (25%)

Partner with the media-buying team to make creative the growth lever — while keeping the rest of the portfolio consistent with what performance data teaches you.

- The concept → test → iterate → scale system across thousands of live ads: how ideas enter, how you test cleanly, what graduates to scale.
- Fatigue management across a large live ad account — how you keep the account fed without drowning in near-duplicates.
- Turning performance data into creative insight (not just more variations of the winner) — give a concrete example of a read you'd act on, and how that insight would flow upstream into brand, OOH, or ambassador work.
- Working with the media buyers: the operating cadence, who owns what, and how you handle the time-zone reality day to day.

---

## Part 3 — The Creative Work: Show Us, Don't Tell Us (20%)

Show range across the portfolio — actually make something.

- Produce 5 net-new ad concepts for Daily Ultimate Essentials — each with hook, angle, format, target, and a rough storyboard/mock (drawn, Figma, or annotated screenshots).
- Show range across the portfolio: at least one lo-fi creator-style concept, one higher-production idea (TV/brand film or OOH-worthy), and one built around an ambassador.
- One paragraph on why these, and which you'd bet on scaling first.

*(We want to see creative judgment and craft, not just management.)*

---

## Part 4 — Leading the Editor Team & Operating Model (15%)

This role lives or dies on whether the editor team performs. Show us how you lead one.

- Managing the 5–10 editors: how you structure the team (by channel, by format, by pod?), set the quality bar, run feedback and review, and develop editors' craft over time. What does a great week look like for an editor on your team?
- Performance management: how you spot and fix underperformance, how you retain your best editors, and how you onboard/level new ones.
- Your first three hires and why.
- The RACI between the global content org and channel/media/social teams — who decides, who delivers.
- A realistic weekly operating rhythm across global time zones that keeps decisions fast (be specific about the overlap hours and what happens in them).

---

## Part 5 — AI-Native Production at Scale (10%)

Real workflow, real prompts — not a tool list.

- How you embed AI to help hit 5,000 assets/month: ideation, variant generation, localization, QA/claims-check, and tagging/analysis. Show 3–5 of your actual prompts with outputs.
- Where AI must not replace human craft, brand judgment, or health-claim sign-off — especially on TV, OOH, and ambassador work.

## What We're Looking For

- Systems thinking + genuine creative taste — a builder and a maker.
- Portfolio range: comfort running paid social, TV, OOH, ambassador and organic content as one system, not silos.
- A real people leader: evidence you can manage, develop, and retain a team of editors — not just brief freelancers.
- Performance-creative fluency: testing, fatigue, attribution, scaling.
- Real craft in Part 3 — you can direct the work, not just brief it.
- A believable operating model across HK/US, not org-chart theory.
- Embedded AI workflow with a clear view of its limits.
- Clear, well-organized communication a busy exec can act on.

---

## What We're NOT Looking For

- Generic "content strategy" decks with no operating system behind them.
- Meta-only thinking that ignores TV, OOH, ambassadors, and the rest of the portfolio.
- Volume plans that ignore quality, claims, or fatigue.
- Team plans that are org charts with no view on how editors are actually led, reviewed, and developed.
- A Part 3 that describes ideas instead of showing them.
- AI tool name-drops with no prompts/outputs.
- Anything that could apply to any brand — show us you understand IM8.
