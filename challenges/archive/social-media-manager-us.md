# Challenge: Social Media & Community Manager — US

> ⚠️ **THE FIGURES IN THIS CHALLENGE ARE FICTIONAL — NOT REAL COMPANY DATA.** The follower, engagement, and cadence numbers below are **invented for this exercise** and do **NOT** represent IM8's actual social metrics. Only publicly disclosed brand facts are genuine.

**Role:** Social Media & Community Manager (US)
**Brand:** IM8
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Time:** 1–2 hours
**Max Length:** 4 pages

---

## Background

IM8 is a premium performance nutrition brand co-founded by David Beckham, built on science from Mayo Clinic and NASA researchers. We sell direct-to-consumer in the US and are scaling fast.

Our current social presence *(all figures illustrative)*:
- Instagram: ~90K followers, posting 4x/week, engagement rate ~1.5%
- TikTok: ~35K followers, posting 3x/week, mostly repurposed Instagram content
- No active community programme or ambassador network in the US
- No presence at US fitness, wellness, or lifestyle events
- Content is produced primarily by our HK creative team — timezone and cultural gaps are real
- Our audience skews 28–45, health-conscious professionals who spend on premium supplements
- We've run influencer one-offs but have no structured ongoing creator relationships in the US

## The Challenge

**You are IM8's first US-based Social Media & Community Manager. Build us a 90-day plan to make IM8 culturally relevant in the US market.**

Your plan should cover:

1. **Instagram Strategy** — Our feed looks polished but feels distant. How do you make it feel alive, US-native, and community-driven without losing the premium brand aesthetic? What content pillars, formats, and posting cadence do you propose?

2. **TikTok Community Growth** — We're not just reposting. How do you build a TikTok presence that's native, engages US fitness/wellness culture, and drives real followers-to-customers conversion? What's your content engine?

3. **Community & Events** — How do you build IM8's physical presence in the US? Think pop-ups, fitness events, wellness expos, local partnerships. What's your 90-day event calendar and how do you turn attendees into customers and content?

4. **US Creator & Ambassador Programme** — Design a structured programme to recruit and manage US-based creators. Who do you target (macro, micro, nano)? What's the incentive model? How do you keep them producing without micromanaging?

5. **Localisation Without Losing Brand** — Our brand DNA is premium, science-backed, cinematic. But the US market responds to different cultural cues than Asia. How do you adapt tone, content, and community engagement for an American audience while keeping IM8's identity intact?

## What We're NOT Looking For

- A generic social media calendar with "post Reels 3x a week"
- Strategies that could apply to any supplement brand
- Influencer marketing 101 from a blog post
- Ignoring the premium positioning (we're not a mass-market protein brand)

## What WILL Impress Us

- Deep understanding of US fitness, wellness, and lifestyle culture
- Named events, communities, cities, and micro-cultures you'd target
- A creator recruitment strategy with specific tiers, incentives, and management workflows
- Content ideas that balance IM8's cinematic luxury aesthetic with TikTok's raw, authentic energy
- Smart use of AI tools in your content production and community management workflow
- Ideas that make you think "a machine could never come up with this"
