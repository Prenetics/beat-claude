## The Challenge

You've just signed your offer. It's Day 1.

IM8 currently spends **~$2.4M/month** on global paid acquisition *(illustrative)*. CAC is **$175**, blended ROAS is **0.7x first-purchase**, payback sits at **~5 months**. New Customer AOV is **$135**, subscription first purchase rate is **~70%**, 12-month LTV is **~2.5x**. Mix today: **Meta ~55% / Google ~22% / TikTok ~14% / other emerging channels ~9%**. Creative refresh cadence is roughly **3,000 ads/month** — produced by a mix of in-house editors (~45%), agency (~25%), and AI tooling (~30%). *(All figures illustrative.)*

You inherit a team of 4 (2 channel managers, 2 in-house editors, no strategist, no AI/automation engineer).

The CEO has given you a clear mandate for the next 12 months:

> **"Double new customer acquisition. Improve CAC. Get payback under 3 months. Take Meta and Google to the next level, unlock TikTok and AppLovin at scale, and be the first mover on whatever channel comes next. Build the most AI-native performance team in DTC."**

Answer the five parts below.

## Part 1: The 30-Day Plan (30%)

### 1A. Operator Note — under 300 words. This is the gate.

Open Part 1 with a short, credentialing note. We read this first. If it doesn't clear our bar, we won't read the rest.

- The **largest daily spend** you've personally managed.
- The **channel mix** at that scale.
- The **CAC trajectory** you drove (before, after, how long it took).
- **What you would have done differently** with hindsight.

If you can't make the scale case in a sentence, this isn't your role.

### 1B. The 30-Day Plan

Sequence your first 30 days to set the stage for hitting the CEO's mandate. We want to see what you'd actually do day-to-day in your first month — not a vague 12-month vision.

### Your plan must cover

- **Sequence & milestones:** How would you sequence the next 30 days? Be specific about phases, the order of decisions, and what you would and wouldn't try in month one.
- **Channel mix shift:** Where does the growth actually come from? Channel-by-channel projection: what % of incremental new customers comes from each channel by the end of 30 days, and why.
- **Creative volume bet:** What's your specific bet on creative volume and variety? How many ads/week, what split between human and AI production, and how do you measure whether the volume is actually winning?
- **The athlete asset:** With Beckham, Giannis, Sabalenka, Bearman, and Messi NIL, IM8 has arguably the most powerful athlete portfolio in DTC. How would you leverage this in your first 30 days? (No, "use them in ads" doesn't count.)

### IM8-specific angles to consider

- We are in **31 countries**. Where do you focus first, and what gets deprioritised?
- The CAC ($175), payback (~5mo), and ROAS (0.7x first-purchase) numbers tell a story about where the leverage is. Read the math.
- The team has no strategist and no AI/automation engineer. What does that constraint mean for what you can ship in 30 days?

## Part 2: The $1M Deployment (20%)

### What to do

You have **$1M of incremental budget** to deploy tomorrow. State exactly where you'd put it, in one sentence. Then defend it.

### Your answer must cover

- **The deployment itself:** One sentence. Specific. ("$1M into AppLovin against US iOS 25–45M" — not "more performance creative across channels.")
- **Why it's the highest-leverage use of $1M right now:** What the ROI math looks like — show the unit economics, not vibes.
- **The trade-off:** What you would NOT do with the $1M, and why.

We want to see how you think under commercial constraint. Multiple deployments disguised as one will be scored down.

---

## Part 3: The Hard Tradeoffs (15%)

### What to do

Pick **ONE** of these four decisions. Tell us exactly what you'd do, why, and what evidence would change your mind.

**(a)** A major emerging performance channel is pitching you on a $250K/month minimum-spend deal at a projected $150 CAC. Meta CAC is creeping toward $200. Do you take the deal?

**(b)** Your in-house editors + AI tooling can produce 800 ads/week at 75% of agency quality. The agency produces 400/week at 100%. Do you fire the agency?

**(c)** Your MMM says Meta is being over-credited by 30% vs last-click. Your CFO is using last-click numbers in the board deck. What do you do this week, this month, this quarter?

**(d)** A new ad platform launched 8 weeks ago. Early adopters are reporting $90 CAC at <$500/day in spend. Your CEO's mandate says "be live on it in 3 months." Your team is already stretched. Do you go live in 3 months, push to 6, or pass entirely?

### What we're looking for

- A decision, not a hedge. "It depends" without saying what it depends on will fail.
- The falsifiable evidence that would make you change your mind.
- Commercial judgement, not optimism.

---

## Part 4: Creative System Design (15%)

### What to do

Sketch the operating system you'd run between channel data and creative output. We want to see how you'd actually run the loop — with AI inside it, not bolted on.

### Your answer must cover

- **Cadence & roles:** Who does what, when. What meetings, what artifacts, what metrics.
- **Human + AI pipeline:** How are AI tools (Midjourney, Runway, Sora, ElevenLabs, Claude, GPT, and whatever else) integrated into the creative pipeline — not as a side experiment, but as the core production engine?
- **Brief-to-ship flow:** What gets briefed when, and how a winning ad gets compounded into more winning ads.
- **A concrete example:** What does "creative learnings as compounding advantage" actually look like in practice? Give a real example from a previous role or a hypothetical week.

## Part 5: AI-Native Operating System (20%)

### What to do

The CEO has been explicit: we are building the **most AI-native performance team in DTC**. Show us what that actually means — both how you operate today and what you'd build at IM8.

This part has **two halves**, both required.

### Part 5A — Three Real Examples of How You Currently Use AI in Your Performance Marketing Workflow

Same three examples you attached to your application. Walk us through them here. For each:

- The tool / stack.
- The exact workflow it replaces or compounds.
- The output (with proof — link, screenshot, Loom).
- The measured impact (time saved, ads shipped, CAC moved, etc.).

We want **proof, not promises**. "We use ChatGPT for ad copy" is not an example — what was the prompt, what was the output, what did it actually do for performance?

### Part 5B — Four AI Systems You Would Deploy in the First 90 Days at IM8

At least one must be an **agentic workflow** — a system that takes action autonomously, not a tool that requires a human in the loop on every step.

For each system:

- **The tool or stack** (named — Midjourney, Runway, Sora, ElevenLabs, Claude, GPT, custom GPTs, Airtable + AI automations, n8n, Zapier AI, etc.).
- **The workflow** it replaces or compounds — and **whether it's human-in-the-loop or agentic**.
- **How you'd measure** whether it's actually working.
- **What could go wrong**, and what guardrails you'd put in place.

### What we're NOT looking for

"We'll use ChatGPT to write ad copy" is not an answer. "We'll explore AI" is not an answer. At least two of your four systems should go beyond copy or creative generation — think performance analysis, campaign QA, anomaly detection, audience research, attribution modelling, or platform-AI optimisation (Meta Advantage+, Google Performance Max, AppLovin AXON).

## What We're Looking For

- **Strategic depth.** Show us you've thought past the obvious. The obvious answer to "double acquisition" is "spend more on Meta." We want to see what you'd do that's NOT that.
- **Commercial rigour.** Real numbers, real math, real tradeoffs. If you cite a benchmark, cite the source.
- **Specificity over surface area.** A 4-page memo with sharp answers beats a 6-page memo with hedges. Vague answers get scored down hard.
- **Builder energy.** This is a build job, not an inherit job. Show us how you operate when there is no playbook.
- **AI-native, not AI-curious.** Every part of this challenge should reflect how you'd actually use AI on the job.

---

## What We're NOT Looking For

- Generic frameworks copy-pasted from agency decks or marketing blogs
- "Test, learn, iterate" without naming what gets tested, learned, or killed
- A six-page memo that says less than a four-page memo would
- "It depends" answers on Part 3 — pick one and defend it
- AI workflows without proof — links, screenshots, or Looms are required for Part 5A
- Strategy that ignores the athlete portfolio or treats it as wallpaper
- Single-market thinking — IM8 is in 31 countries, your plan should reflect that
- Padding, hedging, or anything that reads like a consultant deliverable
