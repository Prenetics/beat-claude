## The Challenge

You are the newly hired Head of Content at IM8. Your first 30 days are a strategy sprint: audit IM8's current content footprint, define the editorial vision, and pitch the **flagship long-form content concept** that will anchor IM8's brand for the next 12 months.

**Everything you need is publicly available.** Visit IM8's owned surfaces. Read the science. Watch what's already been published. Then design something better.

## Part 1: Editorial Vision & Content Strategy (35%)

### What to do

Define IM8's complete content strategy across every owned surface — YouTube, podcast, blog, newsletter, im8health.com, app — and set the editorial vision the team will execute against.

### Your strategy must cover

- **Editorial Vision:** In one paragraph, what is IM8's content trying to achieve, and what does it stand for? What's the editorial north star?
- **Brand Voice & Tone:** Define IM8's voice — vocabulary, cadence, tonal range. Show the dial settings: what we say yes to, what we say no to. Examples required.
- **Content Pillars:** Define 4–6 content pillars (e.g. science of nutrition, longevity & performance, ambassador stories, IM8 inside / behind the science, ritual & habits). Show the role of each, target audience, and approximate output share.
- **Surface Strategy:** What does each owned surface do, and what does it NOT do?
  - YouTube — flagship long-form, athlete features, science explainers
  - Podcast — interview-led? solo? hosted by whom?
  - Blog — SEO-driven? editorial features? both?
  - Newsletter — what's the unique value vs the email programme?
  - im8health.com / app — content-as-product
- **Atomization Workflow:** How does one long-form piece become 20+ derivative outputs? Map the workflow.
- **Editorial Calendar:** What does a typical month look like across all surfaces? Where do tentpoles, launches, and seasonal moments fit?
- **Science & Credibility Strategy:** How do you translate IM8's RCT, NSF Certified for Sport, and clinical formulation story into content people *want* to consume?
- **Ambassador Storytelling:** How do you turn Beckham, Sabalenka, and Bearman into content engines without making it look like a fan zine?
- **KPIs:** What you measure across surfaces, and how you connect content to brand search, branded demand, and acquisition.

### IM8-specific angles to consider

- IM8 competes against AG1, Ritual, Momentous — most of whom rely on athlete endorsements but lack a true editorial voice. What's the wedge?
- 12-week RCT is a moat — most supplement brands don't have one. How do you make it the foundation of a content strategy without sounding like a study summary?
- Ambassador over-reliance is a real failure mode. How do you build a content brand that survives Beckham's absence?
- Habit formation is the retention mechanic. How does long-form content reinforce daily ritual?

## Part 2: IM8 Content Audit + Competitive Benchmarking (20%)

### What to do

Audit IM8's current content footprint across all owned surfaces and benchmark against 3–4 competitors and adjacent brands (e.g. AG1, Ritual, Momentous, Huberman Lab, Athletic Greens, ARMRA, Peloton).

### Your audit should include

- **Surface-by-surface snapshot:** What exists today on YouTube, podcast (if any), blog, newsletter, on-site? What's the cadence, depth, and quality?
- **Top 3 things IM8 is doing well today**
- **Top 5 things you'd change in your first 30 days**
- **Competitive comparison:** Where does IM8 sit on the editorial-quality spectrum vs the brands you've benchmarked? Screenshots and links required.
- **Editorial gap analysis:** Where is the white space IM8 should own? What story can no competitor credibly tell?

---

## Part 3: Flagship Long-Form Pitch (25%)

### What to do

Pitch **one flagship long-form content concept** you would build and ship in your first 90 days at IM8 — something the brand could anchor an entire year of marketing around.

### Your pitch must include

- **Concept name + 1-line elevator pitch**
- **Format & surface:** YouTube docu-series? Podcast? Branded film? Editorial feature series?
- **Why this, why now:** The editorial logic for why this is THE concept for IM8 right now
- **First-episode treatment:** Full treatment for episode 1 / piece 1 — angle, narrative arc, key moments, who's on camera, length, look-and-feel references (linked)
- **Season / series outline:** 6–10 episodes mapped at concept level
- **Distribution map:** How does this flagship get atomised across social, email, on-site, and partnerships?
- **Talent and production:** Internal vs external? What does the production stack look like?
- **Budget order-of-magnitude:** Rough investment range and what we'd get for it
- **Success metrics:** What does success look like at 90 days, 6 months, 12 months?

We'd rather see one fully-developed flagship concept than three half-baked ones. Show us the thing IM8 could win an award for.

---

## Part 4: AI Integration (15%)

### What to do

Demonstrate how you would embed AI across the editorial stack at IM8. This is not theoretical — show your actual prompts, outputs, and refinements.

### We want to see

- **Research & Outlining:** Show your real prompts for researching a topic and structuring an outline. Include outputs and how you refined them.
- **First-Draft Workflow:** Walk us through how you'd use AI to draft a long-form article or YouTube script — what prompts, what context, how you edit the output.
- **Translation & Repurposing:** How would AI handle multi-format atomisation (long-form → social → newsletter → on-site)?
- **Editorial QA:** How does AI help with fact-checking, tone consistency, and copyediting at scale?
- **Prompt Library:** Share 3–5 of your best prompts for content-specific tasks, with notes on when you use each.

We're not looking for a list of AI tools you've heard of. We want to see AI is genuinely embedded in how you work — and equally, that you have a clear point of view on where AI should NOT replace human craft.

## Part 5: Presentation & Communication (5%)

### What we're evaluating

- **Clarity:** Well-organised, easy to follow, professionally presented
- **Editorial taste:** Does the work feel like it belongs in IM8's premium brand world? Would we be proud to publish it?
- **Strategic-editorial balance:** Strategy backed by strong editorial instinct, not one or the other
- **Attention to detail:** Typos, formatting, sloppy work will count against you
