## The Challenge

You have just joined IM8 as a Customer Service Executive.

Customers come to us excited, confused, frustrated, or somewhere in between — and how we respond shapes whether they stay. Your job in this challenge is simple: **handle real customer situations well, spot one thing we could do better, and show us how AI helps you respond faster without losing the human touch.**

We care less about polish and more about tone, accuracy, judgment, and care for the customer.

---

## Part 1 — Handle Real Customer Messages (40%)

Below are four messages a Customer Service Executive at IM8 might receive. **Write the reply you would actually send** to each one.

**Message 1 — Delivery problem (upset customer)**
> "I ordered almost three weeks ago and my Daily Ultimate Essentials still hasn't arrived. This is ridiculous for the price. I want a refund and I'm done with IM8."

**Message 2 — Health and medication question**
> "I've just started blood pressure medication. Is it safe for me to keep taking IM8 every day? I don't want any problems."

**Message 3 — Surprise subscription charge**
> "I didn't realise this was a subscription — I've just been charged again and I didn't want another order. Please cancel it and refund me."

**Message 4 — Adverse reaction**
> "I started IM8 about ten days ago and I've come out in hives across my chest and arms. It's not settling. I've been taking it every morning like the instructions say. Is this normal? Should I push through or stop? I've got three more months on my subscription."

### For each reply, we are looking at:

- Tone: warm, calm, human, and on-brand — never scripted or defensive
- Accuracy: only say things you can support; do not invent policies or promise what you cannot confirm
- Judgment: when to reassure, when to escalate, and when **not** to give an answer (see Messages 2 and 4 — you are not a medical professional, and a customer's safety always comes first)
- Resolution: a clear next step so the customer knows exactly what happens now

Briefly note (one line each) any information you would need to look up or any point where you would escalate internally.

## Part 2 — Spot & Fix One Friction Point (25%)

Look at IM8's public customer touchpoints (website, subscription/FAQ pages, reviews, social comments) and identify **one recurring friction point** customers actually run into.

### Your answer must cover:

- What the friction is, and the public evidence you saw it in
- Why it costs IM8 (frustrated customers, refunds, churn, repeated tickets)
- One practical fix a Customer Service team could ship — and **include one tangible artifact** (e.g. a reusable reply template/macro, a short FAQ entry, or a proactive "before you ask" email)

Pick one and make it real. Do not give us a long list.

## Part 3 — Show Your AI Advantage (25%)

Use AI to help you work through this challenge, then show us the work behind the work.

### Your answer must include:

- Your original prompt
- One weak or generic AI reply (the kind that sounds robotic or off-brand)
- How you corrected or redirected it
- The improved version you would actually send
- One repeatable AI workflow that could help a support team respond faster every day
- The point where a human must take over (tone, empathy, policy, and anything health-related)

We are not scoring how many AI tools you can name. We are scoring how well you steer them and keep the customer voice human and accurate.

## What We're Looking For

- A genuinely warm, human, on-brand customer voice
- Calm de-escalation and clear ownership of a problem
- Sound judgment on sensitive requests — especially knowing not to give medical advice
- Accuracy and honesty over made-up policies or over-promising
- One practical, shippable improvement backed by real evidence
- Evidence that AI makes you faster without making you sound like a bot

---

## What We're NOT Looking For

- Robotic, copy-paste replies that ignore how the customer feels
- Giving medical or dosage advice, or making health claims
- Telling a customer to "push through" a possible adverse reaction instead of putting their safety first
- Inventing refund, shipping, or subscription policies you cannot verify
- Over-promising ("full refund immediately!") without a sensible next step
- A long list of complaints with no chosen fix
- Polished AI text you did not challenge or make human
- Replies that could belong to any brand
